# NYC Mesh Inventory

A web app for tracking [NYC Mesh](https://www.nycmesh.net/)'s hardware: what
we have, where it is, and who has taken it to an install. It replaces the
Google Form and Sheet the volunteers use today.

Volunteers scan the QR labels on stock with their phone, gather several items
into one batch, and send the whole batch at once. Every movement is recorded,
from shelf to volunteer to install, so the counts can be trusted and corrected
without editing history.

Nothing is usable by volunteers yet.
